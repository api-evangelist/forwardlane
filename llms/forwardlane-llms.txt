# ForwardLane — Signal Studio

> Signal Studio is the decision intelligence platform by ForwardLane. It transforms enterprise data into auditable actions — not dashboards. Ask in natural language, get Next Best Actions with complete audit trails, in seconds instead of weeks.

## What is Signal Studio?

Signal Studio is an all-in-one decision intelligence platform that combines:
- Semantic data layer mapping raw data to business concepts
- Multi-agent AI orchestrating complex queries
- Natural language conversational interface
- Visual no-code signal builder
- In-database AI processing (data never leaves your environment)
- Complete audit trails for compliance

## What is Decision Velocity?

Decision Velocity is the speed from question to auditable action. Traditional BI delivers dashboards in 6-12 months. AI analytics delivers insights in 3-6 months. Decision Intelligence (Signal Studio) delivers decisions + actions in under 1 month.
